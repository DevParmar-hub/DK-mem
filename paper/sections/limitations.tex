\paragraph{Lexicon and Marathi review.} The v1 lexicon has 15 Hindi entries and is context-free, so it can mis-tag a word used outside its intended sense:
the Hindi reflexive \emph{apne aap} is tagged as the polite pronoun \emph{aap} (no frozen benchmark text contains this phrase). The same limitation applies more broadly
to kinship terms used as address for non-relatives and to register terms that also mark number. Marathi \emph{tumhi}, for example, is both a polite and a plural ``you''.
No Marathi or Gujarati lexicon entries are part of v1; the proposed Marathi entries are unvalidated, and their suffix lists omit some inflected forms and all romanised inflections.
We did not run the Marathi or Gujarati items, so we make no claim about performance in those languages. The Marathi specifications, distractor turns and proposed lexicon entries
received an in-team review in which the annotations were drafted by an AI assistant rather than by a native speaker; this is neither independent nor full native validation, and the 30 distractor turns have only this limited check.
Repaired items and uncertain labels (for example, kinship same-pairs that rely on an unstated single-relative assumption) need native-speaker confirmation before any future benchmark release.
